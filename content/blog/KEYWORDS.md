# Blog keyword plan

Market: **USA first, UK second.** Write in US English; mention UK where it matters.

## How this research was done (and its limits)

- The Ahrefs connection on this account doesn't include API data ("Insufficient plan"), so there are **no search-volume or difficulty numbers** here.
- Keywords come from **Google autocomplete** for the US (`gl=us`) and the UK (`gl=uk`), checked 23 Oct 2026. Autocomplete only shows phrases people really type, so every keyword below has real demand. It doesn't tell you how much.
- Before you write a new post, check volume and difficulty in Ahrefs Keywords Explorer, Google Keyword Planner or Search Console. Pick keywords a small, new site can realistically rank for.

## Rules for every post

1. **One main keyword per post.** Use it in the slug, the SEO title (≤60 chars, keyword first), the first paragraph and at least one H2.
2. **3–5 related keywords.** Work them into H2s, H3s and the FAQ, and keep the wording natural.
3. **900–1,500 words.** Every section should answer the question. Don't pad.
4. **Meta description ≤155 chars.** Say what the reader gets.
5. **2–4 internal links.**
   - Link to one service or product page.
   - Link to 1–2 other posts.
   - Link to Book a Call or Start Project.
6. **FAQ with 3–5 questions**, phrased the way people search. It becomes FAQPage structured data automatically.
7. **Only real numbers.** Don't make up statistics or studies.
